## Lean thinking ?
Lean Thinking proposes systematically identifying and eliminating the waste generated when creating or evolving a service.

## 5 Lean principles

- Value : Created by product and defined by customer
- Value stream : How value is produced ( End to end flow)
- Flow : To provide constant value flow instead of just manufacturing things in lots
- Pull : Don't pull work until it is needed
- Perfection : Continuous improvement

## Lean principles in software development

1. Eliminating waste
    - Transportation waste: Unnecessary movement of people, information, or data
    - Inventory waste: Accumulation or excess of work in progress, unfinished code, or unnecessary documentation
    - Motion waste: Unnecessary or inefficient physical movements of individuals or equipment
    - Waiting waste: Delays or idle time
    - Overproduction waste: Creating more work, features, or code than required
    - Overprocessing waste: Performing tasks or activities that are not necessary or provide little value
    - Defect waste: Detecting, fixing, or rework due to defects or bugs
2. Build Quality in
    - Putting quality in center of dev work. Ie preventing bugs before they occur through QA and automated tests
3. Create Knowledge
    - Ie create standards and best pracitces to produce quality product
4. Defer commitment
    - Ie , to make a decision with as much contet as possible
5. Deliver value fast
    - By delivering faster one gains context on received feedback quickly
6. Respect People
7. Optimize the whole
