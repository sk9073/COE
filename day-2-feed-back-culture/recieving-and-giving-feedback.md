### Rules for recieving feedback

- Listen , The focus is on what the giver see/hear/think/assume and not on what you intended.
- No Puppeteeiring , Dont try to control the conversation. Better to just go with the flow
- Dont defend or explain. Listening shows accountability
- Ask for clarifying questions , so that it helps on getting the feedback better without mis interpretation
- Need not make promises on the spot
    - Its time for understanding , not for resolution
- Thank the feedback giver

### Advice for giving feedback

- Check your emotional state and motivation
    - The times when we are most motivated to express ourselves are often not the best times to do so.
    - What are you hoping will come of the feedback? Do you expect to feel better for having expressed yourself, or will it produce a better working relationship?
- Talk privately
- Have a plan for the conversation
    - Situation
    - Behaviour
    - Impact
    - Next
- Dont let it be all about correction.
    - Else it would make the reciever feel like they may never be good enough for you.
- After giving feedback , it is not necessary for the receiver to enact on it. The feedback you’ve given them is just information
