## Remote working at incubyte

### Remote working at incubyte follows the below princples

-  Flexibility with Accountability
-  Your Time, Your Responsibility
    - ( Does not mean erratic hours )
-   Ownership and Autonomy
    - Since no one looking over your shoulders; It is expected that you to own your outcomes
-  Proactive Communication is Key
-  Purpose-Driven Meetings
-  “Videos On” for Virtual Interaction
-  Self-Managed and Socially Connected Teams
-  A Career You Control

### Remote working at incubyte does not follow the below princples

- Unresponsiveness
- Siloed Work
- Moonlighting
- Irregular Hours
    -‍“Flexible, yes. Inconsistent, no.”
- Constant Availability
    -Balance is the key

```
Remote work is not just a policy; it’s a mindset. We trust you to bring your best, and we’ll do the same for you.
```
