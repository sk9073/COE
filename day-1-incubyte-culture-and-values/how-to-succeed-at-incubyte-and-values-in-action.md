## How to Succeed at Incubyte

### Relentless Pursuit of Quality with Pragmatism

- Practice Test-Driven Development (TDD), because finding bugs is way less fun than preventing them
- Implement CI/CD pipelines so robust they could survive a zombie apocalypse
- Engage in pair programming – because two heads are better than one, unless you're a hydra
- Refactor code regularly – think of it as giving your codebase a spa day
- Conduct code reviews that are thorough enough to make Sherlock Holmes proud

### Extreme Ownership

- Pick up tasks from the backlog like they're free tacos – enthusiastically and without hesitation
- Respond to messages faster than you can say "Have you tried turning it off and on again?"
- Understand the big picture – you're not just writing code, you're crafting digital dreams!

### Proactive Collaboration

- Participate in BDD sessions with the enthusiasm of a kid in a candy store
- Engage in mob programming – it's like a flash mob, but with more keyboards and less dancing
- Share your knowledge like you're a tech version of Oprah: "You get a tip! You get a tip! Everybody gets a tip!"


### Others

- Set aside learning time each week 
- Share interesting finds with colleagues
- Attend conferences and meetups
- Be a consultant, not just a code monkey – offer solutions, not just nods and grunts
- Check in with clients regularly – be attentive, not stalker-ish
- Anticipate client needs like a psychic octopus predicting World Cup winners
- Escalate issues early – be the Paul Revere of potential problems
- Treat internal stakeholders like VIPs – minus the red carpet, unless you're feeling fancy
- Improve processes continuously
- Monitor systems vigilantly
